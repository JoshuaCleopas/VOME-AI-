How the test worked

Every model got the same written brief and the same reference images, except the motion graphics round, which used words only. Each film is the model's first complete result: no retries, no repairs. Models that can't make 30 seconds in one go made their film in parts, each part starting from the last frame of the one before.

All 64 generations ran on Higgsfield, about $380 worth of credits, which Higgsfield provided as the sponsor of the video. The scores are my own.

Five scores, each out of 5

25 points a round, 100 overall. 0 means missing or unusable, 3 means it mostly works with noticeable problems, 5 means it meets the brief without a meaningful issue.
